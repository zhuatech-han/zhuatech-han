## Starting a project

1. **Explain the business problem:** the intended result and current systems or materials.
2. **Define the first scope:** feasible options, deliverables, schedule and budget basis.
3. **Develop and validate in stages:** milestones, tests and feedback.
4. **Deploy and hand over:** agreed source, documentation, deployment materials and support scope.

A complete requirements document is not needed to begin discussing a concrete scenario.
